About The Organization

Rooted in purpose, built for technical growth

Sagi Rama Krishnam Raju Engineering College was established in 1980 and is recognized by the college itself as one of the earliest self-financing engineering colleges in Andhra Pradesh. The institution was founded with a clear goal: empowering rural students through technical education in a strong academic environment.

The SRKR campus spans 30 acres in Bhimavaram and presents itself as an autonomous institution with state-of-the-art facilities for science and technology, a research-oriented culture, and an inclusive teaching-learning environment. This clubs portal extends that same ecosystem by connecting students with communities for innovation, leadership, creativity, and peer learning.
